# The incubator ladder above Nirmaan

**Written 2026-08-30. Every program fact below was checked against the live site that day, and the
source is named. Where a site would not load, it says so instead of guessing.**

Nirmaan is submitted (29 Aug). The goal now is the tier above it: T-Hub, CIIE.CO, NSRCEL, IITM
Incubation Cell, Villgro, FITT IIT Delhi. This page is what that tier actually requires, which of
the six are real targets, which are not, and the order to take them in.

**The one-line answer:** four of the six are gated on things Raha does not have yet (a registered
company, DPIIT recognition, revenue), two of the six are the wrong sector entirely, and the
highest-probability route to a serious incubator is the ladder you are already standing on. Spend
**two hours this week**, not two weeks.

## 2 · The three gates that decide all six

Every program above Nirmaan is a filter on the same three facts. None of them are about the pitch.

### Gate 1 — the entity, and why it is not a paperwork problem

Raha has **no registration of any kind**, deliberately: the Nirmaan application says so, because
p.9 of the Pratham guidebook makes it an eligibility answer. That was correct for Nirmaan and it is
disqualifying almost everywhere above it.

- **DPIIT recognition requires an incorporated entity** (Pvt Ltd, LLP, or registered partnership).
  No entity, no DPIIT number.
- **No DPIIT number, no government-backed seed money.** That is the rail underneath most incubator
  grant offers.
- **And the biggest one is already shut.** The Startup India Seed Fund Scheme portal carries a
  standing notice: *"Final Notice (Extended): Last date for startups to apply under SISFS
  31 May 2026."* (seedfund.startupindia.gov.in, read 30 Aug 2026). SISFS is the ₹20L
  proof-of-concept / ₹50L commercialization scheme that flows through exactly these incubators. For
  this cycle it is closed. Nobody is going to mention this when they tell you to "apply to T-Hub".

**Do not incorporate this month anyway.** Two reasons, in order:

1. **Nirmaan's own agreement may govern it.** The Nirmaan FAQ lists *"Can Pre-Incubatees use IIT
   Madras address for company registration?"*, *"Is prior approval required for incubation or
   company registration?"*, and *"What happens if the startup incubates within the IITM ecosystem?"*
   (nirmaan.iitm.ac.in, read 30 Aug 2026). Read those three answers in the Pre-Incubation Agreement
   before filing anything. Incorporating outside the ecosystem without approval is the kind of
   mistake that is expensive precisely because it looked like initiative.
2. **A company with ₹0 revenue is a liability, not an asset.** It brings annual filings, an auditor,
   a bank account, and a two-year DPIIT clock that starts ticking while there is nothing to show.
   Incorporate when there is revenue to put through it, or when a program requires it as a condition
   of an offer already made.

### Gate 2 — revenue

The programs worth having say this openly. NSRCEL's Fintech CoE eligibility: *"Revenue Generating,
must have existing customer base"*, *"In seed to pre Series A stage or Recommended by your
investor"*. T-Angel: *"preparing revenue-generating startups for their first investment."*

Raha is at ₹0 with 0 customers and 5 DMs sent (`python3 scripts/pipeline.py stats`, 30 Aug). **The
first paid ₹2,000 is worth more to these applications than any amount of writing.** It moves Raha
from the pile that says "pre-revenue idea" into the pile that says "early revenue, one customer,
knows exactly who the second is". That is the single highest-leverage sentence in a 2027 application
and it costs one sale, not one deck.

### Gate 3 — where you physically are

This one is not on any website and it decides more than the other two.

If the Razorpay bet lands (Nostro, Track 04, closes 5 Sep: in-person Bangalore from September, 6
months), then from October to March you are employed full time in Bangalore. That is compatible with
exactly one of these six, and it is not the one on your campus:

| Program | Base | Compatible with Bangalore, full time? |
|---|---|---|
| NSRCEL | IIM Bangalore | **Yes.** Same city. Cohort programs are evenings and weekends by design. |
| T-Hub | Hyderabad | Lab32 is a 100-day market-readiness program. Assume it wants you in Madhapur. |
| Nirmaan / IITMIC | IIT Madras, Chennai | Pratham is 6 months on campus with a workspace at the Sudha & Shankar Innovation Hub. **Being in Bangalore is a real conflict**, and it is a conflict with the one program that has already accepted your application. |
| CIIE.CO | IIM Ahmedabad | Unknown until the site loads. |

**Say this out loud before 5 Sep, not after:** if Nirmaan selects you and Razorpay selects you, they
are not both doable at full weight. The answer is probably still yes to both (Pratham's obligations
are mentorship, reviews and milestones, not attendance), but find out from Nirmaan what physical
presence Pratham actually requires **before** you are choosing under pressure. One message to the
programme manager. Ask it in October as a selected pre-incubatee, not in September as an applicant.

## 3 · The ladder you are already on, which nobody mentions

The image lists "IIT Madras Incubation Cell" as a separate destination. It is not separate from
Nirmaan. It is what Nirmaan feeds:

> **Pratham** (6 months, ₹2 lakh seed grant) → **Akshar** (6 months, ₹5 lakh for MVP, pricing, sales)
> → **IITMIC**. Nirmaan's own numbers page counts *"Startup incubated at IITMIC"* and
> *"Startup Registered in MCA"* as outcome metrics. (nirmaan.iitm.ac.in, 30 Aug 2026)

So the route to one of the six most competitive incubators in the country runs through an
application you already submitted, into a program that pays ₹7 lakh across twelve months, on a
campus where you are already a student.

**Expected value ranking, plainly:**

1. **Nirmaan Pratham → Akshar → IITMIC.** Application cost: zero, already spent. Probability: the
   only one where you have a live application, a shortlisting at the 22 Aug VentureArch sprint, and
   twenty accepted LinkedIn connections inside the program. Payout: ₹7L and an IITMIC path.
2. **NSRCEL Launchpad.** Sector-agnostic, explicitly for idea/prototype/MVP stage, top 2 incubatees
   get a ₹5 lakh grant. The only program on this list whose stated eligibility Raha meets **today**.
   Closed now. Register interest.
3. **NSRCEL Fintech CoE / T-Angel / CIIE.** Real fits, wrong month. Revenue-gated.
4. **T-Hub Lab32.** Good program, no visible open cohort, Hyderabad, and it wants market readiness.
5. **Villgro, FITT.** Wrong sector, wrong instrument.

The thing to notice: **items 1 and 2 cost you nothing you have not already spent.** Items 3 through 5
cost weeks. That asymmetry is the whole plan.

## 5 · Per-program dossier

Everything here was read on 30 Aug 2026 from the source named. Program cycles change; re-check the
window before writing anything long.

### NSRCEL (IIM Bangalore) — the best structural fit

- **Launchpad** (nsrcel.org/launchpad): 5-month flagship, **sector-agnostic**, for idea / prototype
  / MVP stage founders. Highlights: cohort learning, 1:1 mentoring, **top 2 incubatees awarded ₹5
  lakh grant**, startup kit with AWS / RazorpayX / Stripe / Zoho credits. Selection is five steps:
  application, review, conversation with the NSRCEL team, **mentor panel pitch**, onboarding.
  Eligibility asks for early stage, founder mindset, **problem clarity through strong validation**,
  and disruptive thinking. Page state on 30 Aug: *"Applications for this cohort are closed"*, with a
  **Register Your Interest** form live above it.
- **Fintech Centre of Excellence** (nsrcel.org/fintech-centre-of-excellence): with the Government of
  Karnataka. Sector list names **RegTech**, **risk and compliance infrastructure**, and MSME
  platforms. Offers regulatory sandbox access, regulator AMAs, bank and corporate partnership
  connects. Eligibility: **revenue generating with an existing customer base**, seed to pre-Series A.
- **Read on Raha:** Launchpad's four eligibility lines are almost a description of the FIRA research.
  "Problem clarity demonstrated through strong validation" is 28 outreach conversations and a
  three-tier rail finding nobody else has documented. The mentor panel pitch is the same format as
  the 22 Aug VentureArch pitch, which means the monetization question gets asked again, and this time
  the LUT-and-FIRA cadence answer exists.
- **Action:** interest form now. Full application when the cohort opens. Fintech CoE after revenue.

### T-Hub (Hyderabad) — good program, no open door

- Flagship programs (t-hub.co/programs): **Rubrix** (product readiness, for the idea-to-product gap),
  **Lab32** (100-day market readiness, 11 cohorts, 286 startups), **T-Angel** (100-day, prepares
  **revenue-generating** startups for their first investment).
- Currently listed "ongoing" programs are corporate and challenge-led: Maruti Suzuki Mobility, MATH
  Nuage (AI/ML), Honda deep tech, Boeing Build 5.0, Youth Co:Lab, AIC SpaceTech, Kotak BizLabs. **No
  fintech or compliance track is open.** The Lab32 and T-Angel pages returned empty on 30 Aug.
- **Read on Raha:** Lab32 is the right rung eventually. T-Angel is the right rung after revenue.
  Neither has a visible window, so this is a notification signup, not an application.

### IIT Madras Incubation Cell — reached through Nirmaan, not around it

- incubation.iitm.ac.in was serving a **"Coming Soon / website construction in progress"** placeholder
  on 30 Aug 2026, so there is no public application page to work from.
- The route is the Nirmaan ladder in §3. Nirmaan tracks IITMIC incubation as its own success metric,
  which means the program is set up to push you there.
- **Action:** none, and that is the point. This one is earned inside Pratham.

### CIIE.CO (IIM Ahmedabad) — cannot verify

- **ciie.co and www.ciie.co did not resolve from this machine on 30 Aug 2026** (connection failure,
  both curl and headless Chromium). bharatinclusion.com redirects to bharatinclusion.ciie.co, on the
  same unreachable host.
- What is known independently: CIIE.CO runs the **Bharat Inclusion Initiative**, which is fintech and
  financial-inclusion focused, and is the closest thematic fit on the list after NSRCEL's Fintech CoE.
- **Action:** open ciie.co on your phone (different network) and confirm whether the site is down for
  everyone or blocked here. Then re-read this section. **Do not write a word of an application
  against remembered program details.**

### Villgro — skip

- Stated expertise (villgro.org): climate-smart agriculture, healthcare, climate action, inclusive
  livelihoods and entrepreneurship. Impact incubator, impact capital, impact metrics.
- Raha could be contorted into "inclusive livelihoods" by arguing that freelancers keep more of their
  export income. Do not. An impact incubator asks for beneficiary counts and outcome measurement, and
  a compliance tool for the top decile of Indian freelancers will lose that conversation on the
  merits.

### FITT, IIT Delhi — skip

- Structure (fitt-iitd.in): contract R&D, R&D partners, corporate membership, IP management,
  technology transfer and licensing, IPR policy, Research Park, NIDHI CoE, BioNEST, AIC.
- It is a technology-transfer office with an incubator attached. Its instrument is licensing IIT
  Delhi research. Raha is software plus a legal-process insight, with no institutional tie to IIT
  Delhi and no patentable core.
