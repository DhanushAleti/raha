## Notes for the writer — delete before sending

- **The exposure total goes on screen one.** It is what was paid for.
- **Never end a scary block without the route out.** A report that only frightens does not get
  referred, and the referral is the point.
- **Say the Tier 1 send-away early and clearly.** Telling someone the thing they already get free
  is what makes the rest of the report believable.
- **Every figure carries the disclaimer.** Every one. No exceptions, no "as noted above."
- **Never write "FIRC" as the thing to request.** FIRA, e-FIRC or FIRS. "FIRC" is the word that
  marks someone as having half-learned the topic — and correcting it is the thing being sold.
- **`Unknown` is a legitimate verdict**, and an honest one beats a confident guess. Pair it with
  the exact question to ask.
- Send as PDF, in the same thread as the payment, within 48 hours of the money landing.
