# Raha — creator guide

The 10-minute tour of what Raha does for you and how to use it. (Give this to founding creators at onboarding.)

## The one number that matters

The dashboard's **"Set aside ₹X"** is the amount you should currently keep parked for tax: your estimated GST plus the advance-tax installment coming up next, based on everything you've tracked so far. It updates every time money lands. Every figure in Raha is an **estimate — your CA verifies before anything is filed**. Raha never files anything automatically.

## 1. Track your income (2 min per payout)

**Income → Add income** for each payout, or **Upload CSV** for AdSense/Patreon exports. For dollars/euros/pounds, Raha converts at the reference rate for that date — if your bank applied a different rate, just edit it; we store whichever rate was used.

## 2. Match your FIRCs (the money move)

Foreign platform income is usually a **zero-rated export of services** — 0% GST instead of 18% — but only if you can prove the money came from abroad. That proof is the **FIRC** (ask your bank for one per foreign credit; many banks email IRM advices automatically).

**FIRC → Log FIRC**, copy the certificate details in. Raha suggests which income entry it belongs to — one tap to confirm. Entries flip from **✗ At risk** to **✓ Zero-rated**, and the "GST exposure" number on your dashboard drops. That red number is literally money.

## 3. Invoice brands properly

**Invoices → New invoice.** Pick domestic (18% GST, split correctly between CGST/SGST or IGST based on states) or export (zero-rated with the LUT note brands' finance teams expect). Numbers are sequential automatically. **Finalise** locks an invoice permanently — that's a GST requirement, so keep it as a draft until you're sure. Download the PDF and send.

Fill **Settings** first: your name, state, PAN, GSTIN, and LUT ARN appear on every invoice.

## 4. Keep documents in the vault

**Vault** stores FIRCs, contracts, PAN, GST certificate, LUT — encrypted, only visible to you. If a notice ever arrives, this folder is your first responder.

## The calendar you can stop memorising

The dashboard shows your next statutory dates: advance-tax installments (Jun/Sep/Dec/Mar 15) and monthly GST returns (11th and 20th). Raha assumes monthly filing — if you're on QRMP, your CA will confirm your actual dates.

## What Raha is not

Not a CA, not a filing portal, not tax advice. It's the layer that keeps your creator income so organised that your CA's job becomes review-and-sign — and nothing surprises you in March.
