# Lightspeed Office Hours, IIT Madras — 5-6 Sep 2026

**Written 2026-09-02.** Registration form answers, then what to do with the twenty minutes.

A Lightspeed investor on campus, 1:1, free, no screening beyond a Google Form. This is the cheapest
access to a top-tier fund that will exist this year. Two things to be clear about before writing a
word.

**They are not going to fund Raha.** Pre-revenue, solo, unincorporated, ₹2,000 entry price, Indian
freelancer market. That is not a Lightspeed cheque and pretending otherwise wastes the slot.

**That is not what the slot is for.** It is for three things: a category read from someone who has
seen this pattern fifty times, a name in a CRM that ages well, and one specific strategic question
answered by the only kind of person who can answer it. Optimise for the third.

## 2 · The twenty minutes

**Open with the rail finding, not with the company.** "An Indian billing a foreign client is legally
an exporter" is a fact most people in the room do not know, and the Wise/PayPal/Stripe consequence is
a genuine surprise. Surprise buys attention. A company description does not.

**The monetization question will come.** It came three times at the 22 Aug IVM pitch and froze you
twice. The answer exists now and it is one sentence:

> The LUT is re-filed every financial year before the first export invoice, and a FIRA is needed per
> payment, so solving it once does not solve it. ₹2,000 buys the evidence check, and April is when
> the annual seat becomes the obvious buy. The statute makes it repeat, not a subscription trick.

Say it in that order, then stop talking. The failure mode is elaborating past the point where it
landed.

**Ask your two questions with ten minutes left, not two.** The registration form already carries
them, so they may open with them, which is the best case.

**What not to do:**

- **Do not ask for money.** Not a seed cheque, not an intro to one. You are pre-revenue and solo and
  they know both. Asking converts a good conversation into a filed-and-forgotten one.
- **Do not ask for a Razorpay introduction**, even though Lightspeed backed Razorpay and even though
  the buildathon closes 5 Sep. The moment you ask a VC for a job referral you stop being a founder in
  that conversation and become a candidate, and you cannot take it back. If they raise Razorpay
  themselves, follow it. Otherwise it stays a question about the category, not about you.
- **Do not present the ₹1,000-3,000 crore market figure** unless asked directly for market size. It
  is a defensible number for an application form and a weak one in front of someone who does this
  daily. If asked: give the bottom-up version instead, 2 to 3 million foreign-currency earners, a
  ₹2,000 entry and a ₹20,000 renewal, and say plainly which part is measured and which is assumed.
- **Do not hide the zero.** Lead with it in the traction moment. Pre-revenue is expected at office
  hours; a pre-revenue founder who rounds up is the one they remember badly.

**The follow-up is the actual asset.** Send one email within 24 hours: three lines, the answer they
gave to question 1, what you changed because of it, and nothing else. No ask. Then send the 26 Sep
result whether it is a win or a failure. **A founder who reports back an honest failed test on the
date they said they would is doing something almost nobody does**, and that is the version of you
worth remembering in 2027 when there is revenue to talk about.
