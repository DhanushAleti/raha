# Follow-ups — the second touch

Most replies to cold sequences arrive on touch 2 or 3, not touch 1. This is the highest-value
outreach work available and it takes about 20 minutes.

**Send every one of these as a REPLY inside the original thread** — hit reply on your own sent
message, don't compose a new email. Threading gives them the original for context, and it protects
deliverability far better than a fresh cold send.

Rules: short (they already got the long version), add **one new fact** rather than "just bumping
this", and give an easy out. Never guilt anyone for not replying.

## Pace

Spread them out — 8 or so minutes apart, same as the originals. 23 replies fired in one burst from
a personal Gmail is the pattern that gets accounts throttled.

## Then stop

After this second touch, the sequence gets **one** more (day 8-ish), then the thread rests. Three
touches is persistence; five is harassment, and it costs you the referral you might have got later.
