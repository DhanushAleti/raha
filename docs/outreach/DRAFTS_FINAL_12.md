## What changed from the old drafts, and why

1. **Half the length.** ~120 words instead of ~210. These get read on a phone between meetings.
2. **The ask is now a question, not a meeting.** The old drafts asked for 15 minutes — that's a
   calendar decision, and calendar decisions get postponed forever. A question gets a one-line
   reply, and a reply is a conversation. You ask for the meeting in your *second* email, once
   they've already engaged.
3. **The old "free snapshot of 3 creators" offer is gone.** It sounded generous but it was
   homework: pick three creators, get their consent, coordinate them. That's friction disguised
   as a gift. Nobody does homework for a stranger.
4. **Every one ends with a line that invites you to be wrong** — *"if they do, I'm wrong."*
   It's disarming, it's honest, and it is very hard not to answer. That single line will do more
   work than any amount of persuasion.
5. **"I'm building Raha" → gone.** "Building" signals unfinished. You shipped it.
