## When someone says yes

Send the forward immediately, same thread, no follow-up question. **The ask fails when they have to
compose it.**

Then log it: `python3 scripts/pipeline.py log <id> dm_sent`

When an intro lands, it goes to the front of the pipeline and gets the **full process** — same rail
question, same written diagnostic, same ₹2,000 ask. Do not treat a friend's referral as a warm lead
and skip the process; the documented failure is getting neither the sale nor the friendship.
