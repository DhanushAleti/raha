# Discovery call script (30 min)

Goal: qualify hard, diagnose honestly, close to a founding seat or a clean no. You are diagnosing, not pitching, for the first 20 minutes.

## Opening (2 min)

> "Thanks for making time. Here's how I'd like to use 30 minutes: I ask about how your money actually flows for 15–20, I tell you honestly what I see — including 'you're fine, you don't need us' if that's true — and if it makes sense, I'll explain what working together looks like. Sound good?"

## Qualification questions (15–20 min)

**Income shape**
1. Roughly what did you earn as a creator last FY, and what's this year tracking to? *(Qualify: ₹20L+. Below that, be honest — refer them to the free audit tool and part warmly.)*
2. Which platforms pay you, and in which currencies?
3. How many brand deals a year, and who invoices them?

**Current setup**
4. Who does your taxes today, and what do you pay them?
5. What's the last thing they did that impressed you? *(Silence here = unhappy = your opening.)*
6. Are you GST registered? LUT filed this year?
7. When AdSense/Patreon pays you, does anyone collect the FIRC from the bank? *(This is the wedge question — most say no or "what?")*

**Pain + urgency**
8. Have you ever gotten a notice, or had a payment held up over invoicing? What happened?
9. Do you know today what you should be setting aside? How does that number make you feel?
10. What would need to be true for you to say, a year from now, "taxes are just… handled"?

## The honest diagnosis (5 min)

Reflect back what you heard, in their words, three bullets max:
> "Here's what I'm seeing: [e.g., ~₹35L across AdSense + Patreon + 4 brand deals, no FIRC trail, CA treats you like a shop owner, no idea what to set aside]. The FIRC gap alone is potentially ₹[X] of GST you shouldn't owe."

If they're genuinely fine: say so, ask for referrals, end 10 minutes early. This builds the brand more than a bad-fit sale.

## The offer (5 min)

> "Founding Creator seat: ₹20,000 for the year — half the standard price, locked for life, 10 seats total. You get: every income stream tracked, FIRCs collected and matched so your exports are provably zero-rated, GST invoices for every brand deal, a live set-aside number, and filings reviewed and signed by a qualified CA. Nothing files without your approval, ever."

Close question: **"Do you want one of the seats?"** Then stop talking.

- **Yes** → "Great. I'll send a one-page summary and the onboarding checklist on WhatsApp today. We start with your last 12 months of statements."
- **Maybe** → "What's the one thing you'd need to see to be a yes?" Handle exactly that, then re-ask once.
- **No** → "Totally fine. Can I send you the free audit report anyway? And if you know one creator drowning in this, an intro is the best thing you could do for me."

## After every call (5 min, same day)

Log in the pipeline tracker: income band, platforms, current CA cost, red flags found, objection, next step + date. Send the promised follow-up within 2 hours while you're still fresh in their mind.
