# Creator DM scripts

Rules: personalise the first line every time (name a recent video/post — no generic flattery), send 15–20/day, never paste links in message one on Instagram (spam filters), move to WhatsApp/call fast. Target: creators visibly earning ₹20L+ (monetised YT with 200K+ subs, active Patreon, visible brand deals).

---

## Script 1 — The FIRC hook (YouTube/Patreon creators)

> Hey [name] — loved the [specific video/post]. Random but important question: when AdSense pays you in dollars, does anyone collect the FIRC from your bank?
>
> Reason I ask: that certificate is the difference between paying 0% and 18% GST on your foreign income. Most creators' CAs never collect them. I'm building Raha — tax compliance built only for creators — and I'm doing free 5-minute compliance checks for the first cohort. Want me to run yours?

**If they reply "what's a FIRC":**
> Exactly the problem 😄 It's the bank's proof your money came from abroad. Without it, your AdSense/Patreon income can't be treated as a zero-rated export — so you either overpay GST or carry notice risk. Takes 5 min to check your setup: [audit link]

## Objection handling (all scripts)

- **"I already have a CA."** — "Keep them! Raha isn't a replacement — we keep your income, FIRCs and invoices perfectly reconciled and hand your CA clean files. Ask them one question first: 'how was my AdSense income treated for GST last year?' If the answer isn't instant, that's the gap."
- **"How much?"** — "Founding price ₹20,000/yr — half the standard rate, locked for life, first 10 creators only. You're probably paying your current CA ₹20–50K for less."
- **"Is this legit / are you a CA?"** — "Every filing is reviewed and signed by qualified CAs — Raha is the creator-fluent layer on top. And nothing files automatically; you approve everything."
- **Silence after audit link** — one follow-up, 3 days later: "Ran your numbers yet? Most creators who did came out red on FIRC coverage — worth 5 minutes before ITR season peaks."
