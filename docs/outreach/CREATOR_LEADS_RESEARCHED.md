## The finding that changes the pitch

**AdSense pays Indian creators from Google Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd., Singapore, in convertible
foreign exchange.** Confirmed on [Google's own help page](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/3025029?hl=en)
and in the [GST analysis at CAclubindia](https://www.caclubindia.com/articles/gst-implications-on-revenue-share-under-adsense-online-advertising-of-google-30852.asp).

That means **every monetised Indian YouTuber is exporting a service to Singapore** — whether their
audience is in Mumbai or Manchester. Zero-rated only with an LUT filed before the first export
invoice of the FY, plus FIRC/BRC proof.

Three consequences:

1. **Stop qualifying on "do you earn from abroad".** If they run AdSense, they do. The old framing
   ("creators with foreign audiences") was needlessly narrow and cost us reach.
2. **The addressable market is every monetised Indian creator**, not the English-language subset.
3. **It's a stronger opener** because it's checkable in one click, on Google's own domain, rather
   than something they have to take on faith.

Worth putting the Google support link directly in outreach — it converts "who is this guy" into
"huh, let me check that."

## Do NOT contact — residency failures

The lesson from C4ETech (emailed 24 Jul, replied "I live in UAE"): **Indian nationality is not
Indian residency.** Verified exclusions:

- **Kunal Kushwaha** (WeMakeDevs, ~680K) — resides in London, works at CAST AI EMEA
- **Akshat Shrivastava** (Wisdom Hatch) — Singapore/Dubai
- **Gaurav Chaudhary** (Technical Guruji) — Dubai

burned one email on it.

## How to get the contact address

For anyone marked "YouTube About": channel → About → "View email address" → captcha. Ten seconds.
**Never guess an address** — a bounce damages sender reputation for every other email in flight.
