## The rule that makes this fast

You are **not** asking "do you have foreign income." Nobody in a 2028 IITM batch does.
You are asking **"do you know anyone who bills foreign clients."** Their second degree is the target.

That changes the search. You are not scanning your contacts for rich people. You are scanning for
**people who know a lot of people** — which is a much easier thing to spot and a much easier thing
to ask.

## The rule that makes it not-scary

The ask is **one paste**. You wrote the forward for them. That is the entire favour: *read this,
decide if a name comes to mind, forward a thing I already wrote.* Nobody has to vouch for you,
nobody has to owe you anything, and a "no" costs you nothing because you never asked them to buy.

If a slot makes you wince, put the name down anyway and move on. **Wincing at 15 messages is the
thing that has cost this project two months.**
