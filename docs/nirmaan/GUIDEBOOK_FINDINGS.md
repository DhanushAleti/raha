# Nirmaan guidebook — the parts that change what you do

Source: *Nirmaan Student's Guide 2026-27*, 14 pages, read in full 2026-08-29.
Only the load-bearing items are here. Everything else is brochure.

---

## 1 · The eligibility landmine — read this before you register anything

> **"Obtaining any form of government registration may disqualify your startup from the Pratham
> program, as government-registered startups are not considered for eligibility under the current
> guidelines, which are still under review."** (p.9)

**You are currently eligible, and it is because you have not incorporated.** Raha is you, operating
as an individual. No MCA registration, no company, no GST registration in a business name.

**So: do not register anything before the cohort decision, and do not let "looking legitimate"
talk you into it.** The ₹2,000 Evidence Check is unaffected — a sole individual below the ₹20 lakh
services threshold has no compulsory GST registration, and taking payment by personal UPI creates
no registration of any kind. Keep it that way until Nirmaan says otherwise in writing.

This is also worth *saying* in the application. "No government registration" is an eligibility
requirement most applicants have to explain away and you meet by default.

## 2 · The money is a reimbursement, not a grant

> "In a grant fund, the teams are entitled to get the whole amount. But in seed funding, teams would
> have to spend the money required from their pocket and later claim for reimbursements." (p.10)

₹2,00,000 in Pratham. **You front it, then claim it back with a proper invoice.** With a ₹0 monthly
budget this changes what you can realistically use it for — do not build a plan that assumes cash
arrives.

Free AWS credits are separate and do **not** consume the seed fund (p.10). Take those.

## 3 · What cannot be claimed — and one of them is your actual constraint

| Cannot claim (p.13) | Why it matters here |
|---|---|
| **Cost incurred in marketing and promotion** | **Your bottleneck is distribution, and the seed fund cannot touch it.** Do not write a funds answer built on customer acquisition — it reads as ineligible to whoever reviews it. |
| Money spent on skill development or any course | Courses aimed at individual entrepreneurial ventures are explicitly excluded (p.11) |
| Hiring interns for any work | |
| Tools used to *make* the prototype but not part of it | |

**Can** claim: consumables that become part of the prototype; subscriptions and spend related to
product development **with prior approval**; trips that benefit the startup, **pre-approved
case-by-case** (p.9, p.13).

Prior approval goes to `nirmaan@smail.iitm.ac.in`, CC `manager.ie@imail.iitm.ac.in`.
Reimbursement claims go to `finance.ie@imail.iitm.ac.in` and `manager.ie@imail.iitm.ac.in`.
A pro-forma invoice is **not** accepted — it must say "Invoice" and carry a unique reference,
both parties' details, and tax information (p.12).

## 4 · The two terms you are agreeing to

**Equity (p.7).** Nirmaan takes **1% at face value** on formal incorporation, any time within 3
years of graduation, on a fully diluted basis, to IITM. Terms valid up to 3 years while you remain
active. For a pre-revenue solo project this is cheap; know it before you sign, not after.

**IP (p.8).** *"Any IP developed by Pre-incubatees during their Pre-incubation period would be owned
by IITM."* On incorporation it comes back to you on an **exclusive licence at nil cost or a mutually
agreed cost**, and if you do not commercialise within 3 years of the licence, IITM may convert it to
non-exclusive.

**What this means for Raha specifically:** the codebase, the audit scoring logic, and the FIRA
research written *during* pre-incubation sit under this. It is standard for a campus pre-incubator
and the licence-back is the protection — but it is a real term and you should not discover it in
month four.

## 5 · The path, and where the money actually is

```
Apply → Pratham (6 mo, ₹2L, business/finance/market basics)
      → Review committee
      → Akshar (6 mo, up to ₹5L, build the MVP, dedicated mentor, pricing + sales)
      → Ready for incubation
```

Only teams that "fared well" cross from Pratham into Akshar (p.6). **₹5 lakh is the real prize and
it is 6 months away, gated on performance.** Treat Pratham as the qualifier.

**UGFIR (p.10):** stipend support for undergraduates graduating in the current academic year who
founded a startup, *"preferably one that is pre-incubated at Nirmaan."* Not you yet — worth knowing
it exists.

## 6 · Things Nirmaan will not do, so do not ask

- No accommodation, on or off campus (p.11)
- No sponsorship of external events, networking or stalls (p.11)
- No market-research spend — *"individuals should independently approach relevant professionals…
  through platforms like LinkedIn"* (p.11). **The guidebook is telling you to do the warm-network
  motion you have been avoiding.** They will not fund a substitute for it.
