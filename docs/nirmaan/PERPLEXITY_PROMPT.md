## What I already established, so you do not re-research it

**Their scoreboard, from their own homepage.** The metrics on the front page are: active teams,
revenue generated by startups, sectors in focus, total valuation, startups incubated at IITMIC,
teams trained, startups registered in MCA, IPs filed. Those are the outcomes a selected team is
expected to contribute to later, which is why the "potential IP" answer is worth taking seriously
and why a credible path to incorporation and revenue matters more than polish.

**What the programme says it is for.** Mentors guide *"problem identification, validate their ideas,
and uncover customer insights."* The faculty advisor describes helping founders *"refine their
ideas, validate their technology"* and move *"from concept to a strong, scalable venture."*
Pratham takes a team with an identified problem and no validation and produces validation. Being
pre-revenue is the entry condition, not the disqualifier.

**Funding.** ₹2 lakh in Pratham, up to ₹5 lakh in Akshar for teams that "shine". The website calls
the ₹2 lakh a *seed grant*; the guidebook p.10 says it is a **reimbursement and explicitly not a
grant**. The guidebook governs — assume you front the money.

**Terms.** 1% equity at face value on incorporation within 3 years of graduation. IP created during
pre-incubation is owned by IITM, licensed back exclusively at nil or agreed cost on incorporation,
convertible to non-exclusive if you do not commercialise within 3 years.

**Eligibility.** Guidebook p.9: government registration may disqualify you. You have none.
