## Brief description of that experience — **the answer that decides this application**

> Before Raha I ran eleven startup ideas through a written scoring rubric — pain severity,
> willingness to pay, time to first revenue, solo feasibility, competition and defensibility, scored
> out of 50, with the competitive landscape checked against live web research rather than assumed.
> Ten were killed on paper. STASH died because consumer investing needs RBI and SEBI licensing I
> cannot get as a student. Shero died because capital-heavy mobility already has a graveyard —
> Shuttl, Zipgo. Saved Reels died because five products shipped into that space in 2025 while I was
> still thinking about it.
>
> The one that scored 42 out of 50 became Raha. It won on a specific finding: creators earning ₹20
> lakh and above already pay chartered accountants ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 a year for this exact scope
> and are unhappy with the result, so the willingness to pay did not have to be created. I then went
> and checked that with 28 real conversations, and wrote up what came back as a paper — attached.
>
> Alongside that I have shipped and open-sourced work that has nothing to do with Raha, because it
> is where the engineering came from. **NEXUS**, a provenance-aware memory system for AI agents,
> written in Python and clean under `mypy --strict`. **gpt2-induction-heads**, a mechanistic
> interpretability study that identifies L5H5 as the strongest induction head in GPT-2-small and
> verifies it by ablation. Thirty-seven repositories on GitHub, five of them active.
>
> The one I would actually want you to look at is **project-phi**. I spent weeks testing whether
> golden-ratio retracement structure is statistically special in price series against matched
> controls. It is not, and worse, my own method was broken: it returned a false positive on all
> thirteen registered null processes across 1,950 simulations, and I was able to prove why —
> Jensen's inequality on a convex distance functional guarantees a positive result against a
> symmetric control set for any distribution at all, null or otherwise. I wrote that up, recorded
> the verdict as NOT CLEARED, and stopped.
>
> I am putting a failure in an application on purpose. It is the most useful thing I can tell you
> about how I will behave inside Pratham: when the evidence says the idea is wrong, I would rather
> find out and write it down than keep going. Raha is currently at ₹0 revenue and 0 customers, and I
> have said so in every answer on this form for the same reason.
>
> GitHub: **github.com/DhanushAleti** · Product: **https://raha.software** ·
> Free audit, no signup: **https://raha.software/audit**
