# Tactics filter — second pass, 2026-08-27 (evening)

**What this is.** `research/tactics-filter.md` was written this morning against one question:
*what do I do in the next 30 days to get a paying customer.* Against that question it is correct
and I am not reopening it. This addendum runs the same corpus (`research/tactics-raw.md`, 31 videos,
~180 tactics) against a **second** question that surfaced after it was written:

> *How does this make money over time?*

That is the question the IVM judge asked on 22 Aug, three times, in three forms — "how will you
monetize it," "how can you do it," and a third about revenue. It got "I'm working on it" twice.
The Part 5 pitch video is due 29 Aug and it will be asked again.

**The finding, stated plainly.** The morning filter discards the entire answer, on purpose, in two
lines of its own DEAD list:

- *"Missing: an existing customer base or list"* — killed all 13 retention tactics, the whole email
  masterclass **including the annual renewal fee**, and the proactive downsell.
- *"Missing: capital, scale, or an existing business to analyse"* — killed **client-financed
  acquisition and the 15 money-model mechanisms**.

Both calls are defensible for a 30-day acquisition sprint. Neither is defensible for a founder who
has to describe the business on camera in 48 hours. **The filter answered "what do I do"; the judge
asked "what is this." Nothing in the filter answers the second question, because the filter
deliberately threw away everything that does.**

Six items below. Four are un-kills, one was never filtered at all, one is a correction to how an
already-ranked tactic is being used.

---

## M1 · Be in the reorder business, not the order business — and say so out loud

**Source:** `HORMOZI` — *How to get customers to pay forever*. Previously: not in the filter.

Exact mechanics from the corpus: build recurring **or "reoccurring"** revenue so every past customer
keeps paying; treat each customer as "a node of revenue." The line that matters: *"You don't get
customers to make sales — you make sales to get customers."*

**Why it lands here and nowhere else in the corpus:** Hormozi has to *engineer* recurrence into a
gym or a supplement. **Raha does not have to. The statute supplies it.**

| Obligation | Cadence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| LUT | **Re-filed every FY**, before the first export invoice after 1 April | `raha-customer-market-discovery` §2 |
| FIRA | **Per payment** | ibid. |
| FEMA realisation | **Per transaction**, 9-month window | ibid. |

That is the answer to the doubt recorded against the pitch — *what's the point if the value is
making the tax go to zero.* The tax goes to zero **this year**, and the obligation resets in April.
Solving it once does not solve it. **This is a legal fact about the product, not a subscription
trick, and it should be said in exactly those words.**

## M2 · The annual renewal fee — killed for "no list," but it is the product's own calendar

**Source:** `HORMOZI` — *Learn Email Marketing in 39 Minutes*. Previously: DEAD ("no list").

Exact mechanics: a one-time annual fee priced at 1–3 months' equivalent, charged a year in, so it
never touches front-end conversion; the advertised entry price stays the same. Claimed 8–25% revenue
add at no added cost. (Numbers self-reported and uncorroborated — treat the mechanic, not the figure.)

**Why the "no list" kill was wrong:** the mechanic doesn't need a list. It needs *a recurring
obligation with a fixed date*, which is the one thing Raha has and Hormozi's examples don't. The LUT
re-file has a statutory deadline. A renewal charged at LUT time isn't billing engineering — it is the
product's calendar. It also solves the ₹2,000 → ₹20,000 bridge that `PAINKILLER_AUDIT.md` left open
when it took the annual seat off the front door: the seat is not the opener, it is **what April is**.

## M3 · Ghost products / sacrificial lambs — the best-fitted tactic in the corpus, never filtered

**Source:** `HORMOZI` — *My Best Sales Tactic*. Previously: **absent from the filter entirely** —
not applicable, not adaptable, not dead.

Exact mechanics: cross an item off and send the customer to buy it elsewhere — *"you can get this
cheaper down the street, you don't need it from me."* Acting in their self-interest **first** builds
trust fast, so they buy everything else you recommend. Make the send-away items your lowest-margin
ones. (Claimed close rates of 100% / 80–90% are unsupported single-operator assertions — ignore the
numbers, keep the mechanism.)

**Why this is the highest-value un-filtered item in the file:** Raha already does this. The rail
question in `src/components/audit/questions.ts` sends every Skydo / Karbon / Payoneer user away —
*you already get FIRA free and automatically, you don't need me.* It is currently framed as a
**qualifier**, a way to avoid wasting a DM.

The corpus says it is not primarily a filter. **It is the single most effective trust-building act in
a sale.** And per `research/decision.md`, the one untested variable in this entire business is
*"will someone with this pain pay a stranger with no credential"* — which is a trust problem, not a
pain problem or a price problem.

**Same action, different frame:** every person sent away is not a lost lead. They are the reason the
next person believes him. This should be said in the pitch, in the DMs, and in the public answers —
*"if you're on Skydo, stop reading, you already have this."*

## M4 · "Reveal a problem" magnets work best on problems that worsen while waiting

**Source:** `HORMOZI` — *Watch This To Generate 1000s of Leads*. Previously: A8, kept, but noted only
as *"already correctly built."*

The corpus names the category this mechanic fits best — *"great for problems that WORSEN while
waiting"* — and its own example list is **posture, termites, financial audits.** That is Raha's
category, named explicitly, and the morning filter passed over it because it was reading A8 as a
product note rather than a positioning line.

FIRA exposure genuinely worsens while waiting: the FEMA realisation window is 9 months and closes,
and every additional financial year adds unreconciled history that only backfill can fix. **The audit
is not just correctly built. It is the textbook case of the mechanic**, and that is a sentence for
the pitch, not a note for the backlog.

## M5 · Reverse the damaging admission — "negative BUT positive," never the other way

**Source:** `HORMOZI` — *Sell Anything To Anyone With This Unusual Method*. Previously: A9, ranked #4,
but only as a general "own your flaws" principle.

The corpus is more specific than the filter recorded: **everything after "but" is amplified,
everything before it is diminished.** So the ordering is the tactic, not a stylistic choice.

Applied to the pitch's honesty slide — ₹0 revenue, 0 customers, solo, not a CA:

- ✗ *"The product's live and the research is done, **but** I have no revenue and no customers."*
- ✓ *"I have no revenue, no customers, I'm not a CA and I can't file, **but** I found the one thing
  nobody in this market has priced, and the test that proves or kills it has a date on it."*

Same facts. The first ends on the weakness; the second ends on the reason to believe him.

## M6 · Proof–Promise–Plan, and SPCL, for the recording itself

**Source:** `HORMOZI` — *How to Grow Your Business SO Fast*, *My Actual Social Media Strategy*.
Previously: out of scope — the filter was a channel filter, and there was no video to make.

- **Hook formula = Proof, Promise, Plan** (one sentence each). The recorded pitch opens cold; this is
  the 15–20 second structure that earns the next five minutes.
- **SPCL — Status, Power, Credibility, Likeness.** Credibility is third-party validation, which is
  why the PIB services-export figure and the RBI circular are worth more on a slide than any
  self-description. Likeness is why "I'm a student, solo, no team" is an asset in a student cohort
  and not a confession.

## What this does not change

The 30-day channel, the weekly floor, the dated kill criteria, and every ranked tactic in
`tactics-filter.md` stand as written. This addendum adds a monetization axis the first pass scoped
out; it does not reopen the channel decision, and it is **not** a reason to build anything. M1–M4 are
sentences to say, not features to ship — `research/decision.md` still says product work is stopped
for 30 days, and it still means it.

**Provenance note, in the house style:** every claimed figure in M1–M6 is a single-operator
self-report from an infoproduct seller with an incentive to inflate. None is corroborated. What is
being adopted here is the *mechanism* in each case; the only load-bearing numbers in this document
are the statutory ones in M1, which come from the CA interview in
[`raha-customer-market-discovery`](../docs/Raha_Customer_Market_Discovery.docx) and can be checked
against the Act.
